# Intelligent Medical Inventory Management

## PBL – Month 1

An Artificial Intelligence based project focused on improving medical inventory management using AI problem-solving and decision-making techniques.

### Project Objectives

- Identify problems in medical inventory management.
- Understand low-stock, excess-stock and expiry-related issues.
- Study AI search and decision-making techniques.
- Explore the use of heuristics and CSP for inventory decisions.
- Review existing research related to drug demand and inventory management.

### Month 1 Work

During Month 1, the team:

- Finalized the project topic and problem statement.
- Identified the main requirements and stakeholders.
- Studied AI Unit 1 – Problem Solving Methodologies.
- Studied AI Unit 2 – Adversarial Search.
- Mapped AI concepts to possible inventory-management tasks.
- Reviewed four research papers.
- Prepared the initial system concept and future development plan.
